GameZone
 

Connect Sleeper

Pulls your league, lineup and live fantasy points, so plays involving your starters get flagged as they happen. Read-only and public — no password, no login, and nothing stored outside this browser.

The play feed works without this — skip it and you get every play from every game, just with no fantasy layer.

Settings

Saved in this browser.

Sound
Chime on touchdowns, softer tone on red-zone entries.
Motion
New plays animate in as they land.
Desktop notifications
Touchdowns and your starters, shown even when this tab is in the background. Works only while the tab stays open.
Phone alerts
Bet legs hitting and busting, pushed to your phone even when the app is closed. Needs the app on your Home Screen and the sender in worker/ deployed.

Plays come from ESPN’s public endpoints and run roughly 30 seconds behind the broadcast. Fantasy points come straight from Sleeper, so they match your Sleeper app exactly.
